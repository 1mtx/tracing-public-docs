# VMatrix Integrity

VMatrix Integrity records hashes of your data on a blockchain, so that anyone
can later check that the data existed at a given time and has not been changed
since. Your content never leaves your systems: you send only its hash, and each
client anchors into a registry contract of its own.

## [Anchoring](integration/anchoring.md)
  anchor one hash or many, look a hash up and check it on chain, event types
  and Merkle trees, your registry and its index, errors.

### [Webhooks](reference/webhooks.md)
  signed notifications when an anchor is mined or fails, and how to verify and
  answer them.
